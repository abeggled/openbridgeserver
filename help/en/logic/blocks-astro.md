# Blocks: Astro

Astronomical calculations for time- and daylight-dependent automations.

## Astro Sun {#logic-block-astro-sun}

Computes sunrise and sunset for the configured location (**latitude**/**longitude**) and also
outputs a **Daytime** trigger output that's active between sunrise and sunset — e.g. to control
outdoor lighting based on actual daylight instead of a fixed time.
